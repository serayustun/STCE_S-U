3. Does the causal structure matter?

Each idea was compared with a copy that uses a fake (random) structure. If the real one isn’t better, causality isn’t doing the work.

Current methodNo difference (identical to causal switched off)

4. Options

  • Keep current: most consistent overall. No causal effect to report.
  • Adopt B: best F1 and PR-AUC, best on mammography. Lower recall on 6 datasets. No causal content.
  • Build on E3: the only real causal effect. Costs F1 today, so it needs more work.
